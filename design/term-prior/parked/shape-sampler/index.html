Term-Pool Shape Sampler

Layered patterns whose leaves are replaced by patterns of deeper layers, sampled live in your browser. Every pattern is drawn from the T8 operator prior with the family-affinity urn, independently and with one global set of hyperparameters. Leaves stay empty for now. Change any value and the sample and the analysis update.

Replacement

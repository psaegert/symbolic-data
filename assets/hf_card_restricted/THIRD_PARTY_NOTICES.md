Every dataset in this repo, with its source and the terms at its source (the license pass,
tools/catalog_build/tags/licenses.json in github.com/psaegert/symbolic-data). Each problem also carries them in its
metadata, with srbf's statement:

> srbf republishes these data unchanged so that every run uses exactly the same bytes, pinned by sha256, and does not
> depend on a third-party host that may change, move or disappear. If you hold rights to these data and object to
> their republication, open an issue at https://github.com/psaegert/symbolic-data and they will be removed.

Republishing grants no rights: every dataset stays under the terms of its source. Where a dataset reached srbf through
an MIT, BSD-3-Clause or Apache-2.0 re-host (PMLB, scikit-learn), that re-host's copyright notice is in the table
"Copyright notices" at the end, and the license texts are in LICENSES/.
